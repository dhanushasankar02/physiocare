4 Specialized Post-Op Programs

Directly matching your orthopedic surgeon's operative guidelines.

Total Knee & Hip Replacement

Quadriceps motor re-activation, CPM joint motion, walker-to-unassisted gait retraining.

Spinal Fusion & Laminectomy

Vertebral hardware protection, log-roll bed mobility, and neutral spine core stabilization.

Surgical Scar & Laser Therapy

Class IV laser photo-biomodulation to prevent keloid scar adhesions and drain swelling.

Knee & Shoulder Arthroscopy

Minimally invasive portal incision care, rotator cuff protection, and capsule stretching.
