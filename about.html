Our Founding Story

From a Single Dedicated Treatment Room to a Premier Sports & Medical Rehabilitation Institute

PhysioCare was founded in 2008 by Dr. Sarah Jenkins, PT, DPT, SCS and Dr. Marcus Vance, PT, DPT, FAAOMPT. Having witnessed the rise of high-volume corporate clinics where patients were routinely passed off to unsupervised aides after only 10 minutes with a doctor, they committed to creating a different kind of clinical practice.

Our foundation was built on an uncompromising pledge: every patient receives a full hour of 1-on-1 care directly with a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy. We believe healing requires deep diagnostic attention, continuous manual precision, and a supportive partnership that addresses root mechanical causes instead of masking transient symptoms.

Over the past 18 years, our single treatment room has expanded into a comprehensive 12,000-square-foot medical rehabilitation institute featuring dedicated post-surgical suites, an advanced 3D motion capture laboratory, and an athletic recovery training floor. While our facilities have evolved with modern medicine, our patient-first promise remains unchanged.

“We don’t treat MRIs or X-ray reports; we treat the living, breathing human being who wants to run again, play with their grandchildren without back pain, or regain independence after joint replacement.”

— Dr. Sarah Jenkins & Dr. Marcus Vance, Co-Founders
